Dispatch service for new MC authorities

Getting your MC authority is the easy part. Finding good freight with no broker relationships and no load history is where many new owner-operators struggle. A dispatch service can handle the calls, the negotiating and the paperwork while you build your business.

Why new authorities struggle to find loads

Brokers book carriers they know. A brand-new authority has no history with them, and the best-paying freight often goes to trusted carriers. New carriers also tend to accept the first rate offered because they don't yet know what a lane is worth.

What a dispatcher does for you

We find loads matched to your equipment, lanes and hours of service, negotiate the rate before booking, handle rate confirmations and invoicing, and help with broker setup and carrier packets so brokers can onboard you quickly.

What you need to start

Your MC and DOT authority, a current certificate of insurance, a W-9, and either a voided check or your factoring details. Make sure your authority is active and your insurance is on file before you start booking.

Keep control of your truck

You set your lanes, rate floor and home time, and you approve every load before we book it.

Frequently asked questions

Do you dispatch new MC authorities?

Yes. New authorities are welcome as long as your authority and insurance are active.

What documents do I need to start?

Your MC/DOT authority, certificate of insurance, W-9 and a voided check or factoring details.

How fast can I start running?

Most carriers are onboarded and booking their first load within 24–48 hours once paperwork and carrier packets are in.
