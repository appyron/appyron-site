Reefer dispatch service

Reefer freight can pay better than dry van, but it comes with higher fuel, equipment and risk. A dispatcher who understands reefer loads helps you take the right ones.

What reefers haul

Fresh and frozen food, produce, dairy, meat, pharmaceuticals and other temperature-sensitive freight. Each load has a required temperature and often strict pickup and delivery appointments.

Know the costs before you book

Your reefer unit burns fuel separately from the tractor, so compare rates on a per-mile basis after fuel and maintenance, not on the headline number alone. Our free rate calculator helps you check this.

Details worth confirming on every load

Set temperature, continuous or start-stop mode, pre-cool requirements, lumper fees and who pays them, and detention terms. Food loads can also be subject to FDA sanitary transportation requirements, so keep your trailer clean and your records current.

Protecting you from claims

We make sure temperature requirements are written on the rate confirmation so you aren't blamed for problems you didn't cause.

Frequently asked questions

Do reefer loads pay more than dry van?

Often per mile, but reefer fuel and equipment costs are higher, so compare rates after costs.

Who pays lumper fees on reefer loads?

It depends on the broker or shipper. We confirm it before you accept and get it in writing.

Do you handle temperature requirements?

Yes. We confirm the required temperature, mode and pre-cool details and make sure they're on the rate confirmation.
