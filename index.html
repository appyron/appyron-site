Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much do you charge? ▾
Our dispatch fee is between 5% and 8% of each load's gross, agreed before you sign on — no setup fees and no charges on loads we don't book for you.
What truck types do you dispatch? ▾
Dry van, reefer, flatbed, and power-only. Let us know your equipment and preferred lanes and we'll match freight accordingly.
Is there a forced-dispatch policy? ▾
No. You approve every load before we book it. It's your truck, so you set the lanes, the rate floor and your home time. We bring strong options and negotiate hard.
How fast can I start running? ▾
Most carriers are onboarded and booking their first load within 24–48 hours of signing up, once paperwork and carrier packets are in.
Do you work with owner-operators? ▾
Yes — owner-operators are most of who we work with, alongside small fleets running a handful of trucks.
Can non-US based drivers use the service? ▾
We dispatch carriers authorized to operate in the US, regardless of where you're based, as long as your authority and insurance are current.
Do you dispatch new MC authorities? ▾
Yes. New authorities are welcome. We help with carrier packets and broker setup so you can start booking loads.
What does a truck dispatcher do? ▾
A dispatcher finds and books freight, negotiates rates with brokers, handles rate confirmations and paperwork, and plans routes around your hours of service so you can focus on driving.
What documents do I need to start? ▾
Your MC/DOT authority, certificate of insurance, W-9 and a voided check or factoring details. We'll walk you through the rest.
What is the average truck dispatcher fee? ▾
Many services charge between 5% and 10% of each load's gross revenue. Flat weekly or monthly fees are also common. Read our pricing guide.
Is a flat fee or a percentage better? ▾
A percentage scales with your revenue and aligns the dispatcher's income with yours. A flat fee can be cheaper for high-grossing trucks. Compare what each includes.
Should I pay a setup fee to a dispatcher? ▾
Be cautious. Many reputable services charge no setup fee and only get paid when you get paid.
