Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Appyron ("we", "us") collects through appyron.site and how we use it. Questions? Email dispatch@appyron.site or call +1 346 786 7777.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number (optional), truck or MC number (optional) and your message. Our calculators run in your browser, and we do not receive the numbers you type into them. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, provide dispatch services you ask about, keep business records, protect the site and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the site: Vercel (hosting), FormSubmit (delivers contact-form messages to our inbox), Google Fonts (typefaces) and Google Search Console (aggregated search statistics). These providers may process limited data, such as your IP address, under their own policies.

Cookies

We do not currently use advertising or tracking cookies on this site. If that changes, we will update this page.

Keeping and protecting data

We keep contact messages only as long as needed to respond and maintain business records. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you sent us by emailing dispatch@appyron.site.

Children

This site is intended for business users and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
